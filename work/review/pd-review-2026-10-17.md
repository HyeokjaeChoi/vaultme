# VaultMe Redesign B: product design review

Date: 2026-10-17. Reviewers: Staff Product Designer and YC founder (one pass).
Scope: Figma page `14 Redesign B` (398:76) through `work/compare/*-figma.png` / `*-app.png` (about 70 pairs), the Redesign B docs, and the mobile source (`apps/mobile/src`). This review is read-only.
Method: impeccable `critique` (Assessment A only, because the detector does not apply on a native platform) and `audit.native`, done from source. Mobbin MCP needs login, so I did not use it. References come from my knowledge of the apps. I give no Mobbin links, because I could not check them.

## Verdict

The app matches Figma. The problem is in Figma and in the Foundation. Redesign B removed every container, tint, and accent, but it added nothing to take their place. The result is black text on white, a blue pill, and a grey pill. It reads like a well-labeled wireframe: correct, safe, and with no character. Production apps on Mobbin (Toss, Coinbase, Phantom, Rainbow) are just as flat. They get their quality from four things VaultMe does not have:
- Typographic contrast tuned for the script. Korean needs negative tracking and heavier numbers.
- Strong identity graphics on every row: token logos, direction badges, avatars.
- One grouping device. Toss uses grey bands between sections. Coinbase uses dense rows with icons.
- Feedback motion and haptics.

As a founder, I see a trust problem in five places:
1. A QR code on Receive that says it cannot be scanned.
2. Paragraph warnings on almost every screen.
3. A Review screen made of 10 equal-weight rows.
4. Result screens that look the same as the Review screen.
5. Start and Lock screens that are a wordmark on empty white.

The safety thinking under all this is very good. The visual system does not show it.

| Screen group | Score /10 | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Home | 5 | Clean hero, but the bottom half is empty, there is no recent activity, and the testnet notice is a paragraph |
| Send flow (recipient, amount) | 6 | Closest to production. The keypad screen is good. The recipient screen is a bare text box with no recent or saved addresses |
| Review + risk sheets | 5 | Strong safety logic. The flat 10-row table has no hierarchy. The CTA stays blue in risk states. There is a text overlap bug |
| Results | 4 | Same table as Review with an icon on top. No amount hero, no progress, no end moment |
| Activity + Tx detail | 5 | The list works. Generic arrow icons, no token identity, and received amounts are not green |
| Asset detail | 4 | A copy of Home with a filter. No added value |
| Receive | 3 | A placeholder QR labeled "scan does not work" is the worst trust signal in the app |
| Onboarding / backup / restore | 4 | Correct steps. Pages of warning text. No brand moment. No acknowledgment interaction |
| Settings | 5 | A plain list of 6 rows. No groups, no icons, no account block |
| Lock / update | 3 | A wordmark and one sentence on white. Face ID is not triggered automatically |

## Why it looks wireframe-y (root causes)

1. **Type is tuned for Latin, not Korean.**
   - `Body Small` +0.1, `Label` +0.2, and `Caption` +0.3 px tracking spread Hangul out. Korean fintech (Toss, KakaoBank) uses about -0.02em to -0.03em at every size.
   - With weight capped at 600, the 40 px balance and the 17 px row title have the same "voice".
   - The figures are not visually split from the unit on Home, so `0.05 ETH` reads as one gray block.
2. **No grouping device.** "Flat white, no tint, no border, no card" leaves only white space to group things. On Review, Results, and Tx detail, every row is 48 to 56 px apart with the same weight. The eye cannot find the three blocks: what, to whom, and cost.
3. **Identity graphics are missing or generic.**
   - Activity uses grey circles with Phosphor arrows.
   - Review shows no token logo next to the amount.
   - The recipient has no visual identity.
   - Results use a 40 px outline icon in a muted color.
4. **Too much copy at notice level.** Almost every screen has 2 to 4 lines of icon + Body Small warning text, and all the warnings look the same. When everything is a warning, nothing is.
5. **Weak state treatment.**
   - The disabled Primary (`#DCE8FD` + grey text) looks broken, not disabled.
   - The risk state keeps the blue "확인하고 보내기" on the Review screen.
   - Pending, confirmed, failed, and unknown results share one layout.
6. **No motion or haptics.** `expo-haptics` is not installed. The only motion is the 0.97 press scale and the sheet slide. Production wallets use haptics on keypad, copy, success, and warning, a count-up on amounts, and a drawn check on success.
7. **No brand moment.** The VaultMe wordmark in Display style on white is the whole brand. There is no mark, no color moment, and no illustration. This hurts most on Start, Lock, and Update.

## Top 10 changes (ranked by impact)

### 1. Re-tune the type scale for Korean and give numbers their own voice
- **What:** Use negative tracking at every size. Use a heavier display weight for figures. Split the figure from the unit.
- **Why:** This is the biggest single "looks designed vs looks default" lever. It touches every screen.
- **Spec:**
  - Tracking: Display L 40/48 -1.2 (keep). Display M -0.8. Title 1 26/34 -0.6. Title 2 22/30 -0.5. Headline 17/24 -0.3. Body 16/24 -0.3. Body Small 15/22 -0.2. Label 14/20 -0.2. Caption 12/16 -0.1. Remove all positive tracking.
  - Balance hero: figure 44/52 weight 700, unit 24/32 weight 600 `text/secondary`, baseline-aligned (the pattern on `send-amount` already). Use it on Home, Asset detail, Review, and Results.
  - Row value: Headline tabular. Row subtitle: Caption `text/tertiary`.
  - Allow Pretendard 700 only for `Display L/M` figures.
- **Reference:** Toss home balance and transfer amount screens (heavy figure, light unit). KakaoBank account hero.
- **Change type:** Foundation (`Product B / *` text styles, tokens), then code. This needs an owner decision on weight 700.

### 2. Add one grouping device: grey section bands on detail screens
- **What:** Keep Home and Activity flat white. On Review, Results, Tx detail, and Settings, separate blocks with a 12 px `surface/canvas` (`neutral.50`) full-bleed band. Toss uses this pattern. It is not a card, so it stays inside the Borderless intent.
- **Why:** It gives the eye 3 or 4 chunks instead of 10 equal rows. You get no shadows and no borders.
- **Spec:**
  - New Component alias `sectionBand/height` = `space/3` (12), `sectionBand/bg` = `surface/canvas`.
  - Block inner padding: top 20 / bottom 20, row gap 12.
  - Each block gets an optional Label header in `text/tertiary`.
- **Reference:** Toss transfer confirmation and transaction detail (grey bands between blocks). Revolut transaction detail (grouped blocks).
- **Change type:** Foundation (one Component alias that reuses an existing Primitive). Then Figma screens. Then code.

### 3. Rebuild Review as three blocks with a sentence hero
- **What:** Replace the 10-row table.
- **Spec:**
  1. **Hero:** token logo 48, amount (change #1 style), and one sentence below: "계정 1 → 아래 주소로 보내요" (Body, secondary).
  2. **Block "받는 사람":** full address, mono 16/24, 4-char groups. The first 6 and last 4 characters are `text/primary` 600. The middle is `text/secondary` 400. Add a "수정" link (Label, `text/link`) at the top right of the block.
  3. **Block "보내는 것":** asset, network, account (Detail rows).
  4. **Block "비용":** estimated fee. Total deduction is Headline 600. Max fee goes behind a "자세히" disclosure (160 ms).
  5. **Check status:** one line with timestamp and "다시 확인".
  6. **Footer:** Primary "확인하고 보내기". Remove the separate "수정" text button, because editing now lives in each block. The "취소할 수 없어요 · Face ID" line becomes Caption above the button.
  - **Risk state:** while a look-alike, contract, burn, or needs-review flag is open, the footer button becomes Secondary neutral "위험 확인하기", which opens the sheet. Blue appears only after the user acknowledges the risk.
  - **Fix:** in `review-blocked`, the total and the "최대 …" values overlap ("예상 총 차감액" row). This is a real layout bug in Figma and in the app.
- **Why:** Founders lose users at Review. The user must see "what, to whom, how much it costs" in 2 seconds. Today they must read 10 rows.
- **Reference:** Coinbase Wallet send review (amount hero + to/network/fee blocks). Rainbow transaction sheet (address emphasis). Phantom confirm screen (risk state changes the CTA).
- **Change type:** Figma screen change (Review and all its states). Code follows.

### 4. Make Results an end moment, not a table
- **What:**
  - Hero: token logo 56 with a status badge (16 px circle at the bottom right: check / clock / x / ?), the amount figure, and a title.
  - For pending: a 3-step progress line (제출 → 처리 중 → 확정) that updates in place.
  - Below: one band with recipient (first and last emphasis) and fee. Put the hash in Tx detail only, or behind "거래 정보".
  - Failed: say "보내려던 금액", not "보낸 금액" (the current copy is wrong for failed).
  - Success: draw the check in 400 ms, plus a `notificationSuccess` haptic.
- **Why:** The end of the flow sets how the user remembers the whole flow (peak-end rule). Today, success and failure look like the same table with a different 40 px icon.
- **Reference:** Coinbase send success (big amount + status). Robinhood order status timeline. Toss "송금 완료" screen.
- **Change type:** Figma screens (Results x7). Code. Motion spec note in Figma.

### 5. Use token identity on every transaction row
- **What:**
  - In Activity, Asset detail, and the Home recent list, use the token logo 40 with a direction badge 18 (arrow up-right / down-left on a `surface/default` ring 2 px).
  - Received amounts use the success/gain color (`status/success` text, not blue).
  - Pending uses an amber clock badge. Failed uses a red x badge with the struck amount.
- **Why:** Grey arrow circles make every row look the same. Token logos are free brand color, and they already exist as identity SVGs.
- **Reference:** Coinbase Wallet activity list. Phantom activity (logo + badge). Rainbow activity.
- **Change type:** Figma component (`B/Row` leading slot variant "Asset + badge"). Code.

### 6. Fix Receive: real QR or no QR
- **What:** Remove the placeholder QR with "임시 QR, 스캔 안 됨".
  - **Option A (recommended):** generate a real QR locally from the address. Add a CI test that decodes the QR and compares it to the address. Then it is a checked feature, not an invented one.
  - **Option B:** no QR. Make an address card the hero: account and network chip, full address mono 18/28 in 4-char groups with first/last emphasis, and "주소 복사" Primary. Copying gives a haptic and a toast "복사했어요".
  - Keep the Sepolia-only notice. Make it a network chip next to the title, not a warning line.
- **Why:** A QR that says it does not work tells a new user "this app is not finished". It is the worst trust signal in the product.
- **Reference:** Coinbase Wallet receive. Phantom receive (QR + address + copy). Toss 계좌 공유.
- **Change type:** Figma screen change. Code. The real QR also needs a product decision (constraint: no unchecked QR).

### 7. Give the brand a mark and a moment (Start, Lock, Update)
- **What:**
  - Design a VaultMe logo mark (app icon shape, 72 px).
  - **Start:** mark centered at about 35% height, wordmark Title 1, two-line value prop. Below it, three short value rows with Phosphor icons ("내 기기에 열쇠", "보내기 전에 확인", "테스트넷 전용"). Keep the two buttons.
  - **Lock:** mark centered, "VaultMe 잠김" Title 2 centered. Start Face ID automatically on appear. The button is a fallback.
  - **Update:** mark plus a centered layout. Use the same template for all "full-screen system states".
- **Why:** A left-aligned wordmark on 600 px of white is the most "wireframe" moment in the app, and users see it every time they open the app.
- **Reference:** Toss lock and launch (centered mark + PIN). Phantom launch (mark + brand color). KakaoBank lock.
- **Change type:** Foundation (logo asset, `brand` mark). Figma screens. Code (auto Face ID prompt).

### 8. Add haptics and purposeful motion
- **What:** Install `expo-haptics`.
  - `selection` on keypad key and chip.
  - `impactLight` on copy.
  - `notificationWarning` when a risk sheet opens.
  - `notificationSuccess` on confirmed.
  - `notificationError` on failed.
  - Amount: a 120 ms digit fade or slide when typing.
  - Status changes in place: a 160 ms color crossfade.
  - Under Reduce Motion, keep the haptics and remove the transforms.
- **Why:** Feedback is the main difference between "a native app" and "a screen". It costs almost nothing.
- **Reference:** Toss keypad and transfer success. Cash App amount pad.
- **Change type:** Code, plus a motion note in Figma (`figma-use-motion`) to keep the SSOT.

### 9. Fill Home with real content and compress the testnet message
- **What:**
  - Remove the redundant hero label "Sepolia 테스트넷", because the header already says Sepolia. Put a chip "테스트넷" (amber, Label 12, radius pill) next to the network name in the header.
  - The fake-chain notice becomes a compact chip "테스트 데이터" in the header. Tapping it opens a sheet with the full explanation. Show the sheet once on first launch.
  - Add a "최근 내역" section (3 rows + "전체 보기") under 자산.
  - Backup reminder: one row with a leading icon and chevron ("백업을 아직 확인하지 않았어요 ›"), not a 3-line notice.
- **Why:** Today, the bottom 40% of Home is empty, and the top is 3 lines of disclaimer. Recent activity is the reason people open a wallet every day.
- **Reference:** Coinbase Wallet home (assets + activity). Phantom home. Toss home (compact banner rows).
- **Change type:** Figma screen change (Home states). Code.

### 10. Two notice tiers, and a better disabled state
- **What:**
  - **Tier 1 "Banner"** (page-level or blocking: blocked, error, look-alike, no backup): a tint container `status/*/subtle`, radius 16, padding 16, icon 20, title Headline, body Body Small.
  - **Tier 2 "Inline"** (the current icon + text, 1 line max): for helper notes only.
  - Precheck and backup warnings become acknowledgment rows with checkboxes. All must be checked to continue.
  - Disabled Primary: `neutral.100` bg + `text/disabled`, not pale blue.
- **Why:** The "reduce tinted boxes" decision went too far. Real risk states now look the same as a footnote. Acknowledgment checkboxes also lower the drop-off from reading walls of text, and they prove the user read the text.
- **Reference:** Phantom and Coinbase Wallet recovery phrase acknowledgments (checkbox list). Revolut and Toss error banners.
- **Change type:** Foundation (`B/Notice` Tone x Tier variant, disabled button token). Figma screens. Code.

### Smaller items
- **Recipient screen:** add "최근 보낸 주소" rows under the input (from local history only). This is the strongest drop-off fix in Send.
- **Asset detail:** merge it with Home or add something unique (contract address, network, "받기 주소 보기"). Today it duplicates Home.
- **Settings:** group rows into 보안 (복구 구문, 기기 보안 경고), 알림 (거래 알림, 0원 거래), and 정보 (네트워크, 버전). Use a Label section header and a 20 px Phosphor leading icon per row. Put an account block at the top (account name, short address, copy).
- **Backup reveal:** the 12-word grid is fine. Add a blur-reveal (press and hold) instead of the "탭해서 보기" chip, and a numbered 2-column layout in a `surface/subtle` container.
- **Activity empty state:** the icon is a list glyph. Use the receive icon and a Primary CTA, because the only next step is to receive.

## Keep (already good)

- Send split into one question per screen (누구에게 · 얼마 · 확인). The keypad screen is at production level.
- The safety logic: look-alike diffing with highlighted differing groups, contract-address detection, burn address detection, "형식만 확인했어요" honesty, "다시 보내지 마세요" on pending and unknown, and a separate unknown result.
- In risk sheets, the safe action ("취소") is Primary. Keep this. Do not reverse it.
- Full addresses in 4-char groups in mono. The tabular numbers.
- The single Primary per screen. Neutral Secondary. Blue is not used for success.
- The Dark mode parity (`home-dark` looks right).
- Copy tone: short, friendly 해요체, no fake reassurance.
- The Figma-SSOT discipline and app parity. The gap is design, not implementation.

## Constraints that cap quality, and alternatives that keep the intent

| Constraint | How it hurts | Alternative with the same safety intent |
|---|---|---|
| Pretendard 400/500/600 only | Display figures cannot get heavier than labels, so the hierarchy is flat | Allow 700 for `Display L/M` figures only |
| Flat white, no tint, no card (rule 15) | Detail screens lose grouping and become a long table | Grey section bands (12 px `surface/canvas`) on detail screens only. No border, no shadow |
| "Reduce tinted boxes" for notices | Blocking risk looks like a footnote | Two tiers: tint Banner for page-level or blocking states, inline for helper text |
| No unchecked QR | Led to a placeholder QR, which is worse than no QR | Real QR with a CI decode test, or no QR at all |
| Full address in review/detail | Heavy mono blocks on Results and Tx detail | Keep the full address. Emphasize the first 6 and last 4, dim the middle. This also helps poisoning detection |
| Honest testnet / fake-chain notices | A paragraph on Home, Activity, and Results | A persistent header chip ("테스트넷", "테스트 데이터") + a sheet with the full text + a one-time first-run sheet. It is always visible and always honest, but in a smaller form |
| No real seeds | None. Keep it. "단어1…" placeholders are fine for Figma | — |
| Figma is the SSOT | None. But every item above must go to Figma first, so the Foundation must change first (#1, #2, #10) | Order: Foundation PR → Figma screens → app |

## Suggested order

1. Foundation PR: type tracking and weight 700 for display (#1), section band alias (#2), Notice tiers and disabled token (#10), logo mark (#7).
2. Figma: Review and its states (#3), Results (#4), row badge variant (#5), Receive (#6), Home (#9), Start/Lock/Update (#7).
3. Code: follows Figma, plus haptics and motion (#8).
